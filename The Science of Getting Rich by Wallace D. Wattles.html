What the book teaches

Wattles argues that getting rich follows a method you can learn and repeat, not a gift reserved for a lucky few. He lays that method out in plain steps.

Know what you want

Hold a clear picture of the life and results you are working toward.

Practice gratitude and faith

Keep a steady, thankful frame of mind so doubt does not stall your effort.

Act every day

Do each task as well as you can, in the present, without waiting for the perfect moment.

Give more value than you take

Build wealth by serving others well rather than by competing against them.
